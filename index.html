Operating Manual

Field Instructions

  1. Enter two mutually exclusive actions into the input chamber. The machine can only resolve one binary divergence at a time.
  2. Press Split Universe and wait while the Geneva device emits a photon and registers the quantum event.
  3. When your branch is reported, do exactly what appears on screen. The point is not to decide beforehand; the point is to learn which universe you occupy.

Universe Splitter is a theatrical decision machine. It does not tell you what would be best. It tells you which side of a split you are standing in after the event has occurred.

If contact with Geneva fails, the split is considered incomplete. Retry the sequence or abandon the attempt with Nevermind.

Every successful bifurcation is stored locally on this terminal and contributes to the chart and multiverse statistics readouts.
